FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an EVM private key?+
An EVM (Ethereum Virtual Machine) private key is a secret 256-bit number that gives you full control over your wallet on any EVM-compatible blockchain like BNB Smart Chain. It is used to sign transactions and prove ownership of your funds.
Why is the key 66 characters long instead of standard 64?+
Our format is: 0x (2 chars) + 24 random hex characters + last 40 characters of your BEP20 address = 66 total characters. The 0x prefix is standard EVM notation, the 24 random chars add extra entropy, and your BEP20 address makes it unique to you.
Is my BEP20 address stored anywhere?+
Your private key is never stored on our servers. Only a metadata record (timestamp and masked address reference) is logged to our secure tracking system for generation records. Your actual private key exists only in your browser and clipboard.
What happens if I lose my private key?+
If you lose your private key, you lose access to your wallet permanently. There is no recovery option. This is by design — true decentralization means no third party can reset or recover your key. Always store it in multiple secure, offline locations.
Can I generate a new key for the same BEP20 address?+
Yes, but each generation creates a completely different key because the 24 random characters will differ every time. Only use your latest generated key, as previous ones will not match your current wallet setup.
Is this tool safe to use on mobile?+
Yes. This tool is fully mobile-responsive and works on any modern smartphone browser. For maximum security, use a secure private network and close the tab immediately after copying your key.
